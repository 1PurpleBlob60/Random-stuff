Swordfish™

Swordfish live in ocean

Reasons why Swordfish live in the ocean:

  1. They need water to breathe through their gills.
  2. They find food there.
  3. They live in a wet environment.

Occasional things that can happen to a swordfish

  1. Eat a fish
  2. Chill with other swordfish
  3. Get eaten by shark
